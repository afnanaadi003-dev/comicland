1 Comic History & Evolution

Golden Age to Modern Era
1938 - 1956

The Golden Age

The birth of superheroes. Action Comics #1 introduces Superman, fighting social injustice with superhuman might.

Pioneering Creators:

Jerry Siegel, Joe Shuster, Bob Kane, Bill Finger, Stan Lee & Jack Kirby.

1956 - 1970

The Silver Age

Sci-fi adventures, parallel earths, and scientific marvels revive the comic medium with cosmic wonders.

Pioneering Creators:

Stan Lee, Jack Kirby, Steve Ditko, Gardner Fox, and Carmine Infantino.

1970 - Present

The Modern Age

Deep psychological narratives, graphic novels, human truth, and universal consciousness redefine the boundaries.

Pioneering Creators:

Alan Moore, Frank Miller, Neil Gaiman, Grant Morrison, and modern digital visionaries.
